Evidence-Aware Visual Action Verification & Annotation Auditing

Modern vision models produce confident scores even when the visual evidence is occluded, blurred, clipped, or truncated. ProofCausality AI introduces an evidence-first tri-state decision model (SUCCESS / FAILED / UNCERTAIN) paired with deterministic spatial metrics and targeted recovery directives.

The Four Internal Engines

LabelCourt Visual Annotation Evidence Auditor comparing human masks against AI evidence with IoU and boundary metrics. Emits ACCEPT, REVIEW, or UNCERTAIN.
ActionProof Physical Action Verification Engine analyzing before-and-after spatial state transitions. Emits SUCCESS, FAILED, or UNCERTAIN.
EvidenceCore Shared measurement and uncertainty engine detecting Laplacian motion blur, boundary contact, and visible area occlusion.
ReCapture Deterministic missing-evidence recovery planner providing actionable physical re-recording directives to resolve ambiguity.

ReCapture — Deterministic Missing-Evidence Recovery Planner

Transform uncertain visual observations into actionable physical recovery directives.

Recovery Rule Matrix

BLUR
Hold camera steady until object is sharp.

Stabilize device and ensure adequate lighting to suppress exposure motion artifacts.

OUT_OF_FRAME
Move camera back so full object remains in frame.

Increase distance by 15–30 cm and maintain 70% inner boundary margins.

OCCLUSION
Keep object visible after releasing it.

Withdraw hands completely and capture an unobstructed overhead angle.

MISSING_STATE
Continue recording after action until stationary.

Hold frame for at least 1.5 seconds post-equilibrium.
