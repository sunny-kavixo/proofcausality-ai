# ProofLoop Human Validation Protocol

**Status: READY TO RUN; no independent participant data collected.**

## Research question

Among authentic, rights-cleared visual action cases with a pre-declared claim, does ProofLoop's targeted next-observation directive reduce unresolved evidence more effectively than generic “capture again” guidance, while preserving abstention on cases that remain ambiguous?

## Study design

- Recruit adult participants who provide informed consent and can operate a phone or camera.
- Use a separate, frozen case set not used to author or tune the ProofLoop scenarios.
- Randomize participants and cases to targeted ProofLoop guidance or generic recapture guidance.
- Blind adjudicators to guidance condition when labeling whether the follow-up observation resolves the pre-declared evidence deficit.
- Do not use participant faces, private locations, or identifying metadata without explicit consent; retain only coded case IDs and required responses.

## Per-case procedure

1. Show the participant the initial claim and authentic scene/action.
2. Record the initial ProofLoop verdict and requested observation, without changing the claim.
3. Deliver either the targeted directive or generic guidance according to randomized assignment.
4. Collect one follow-up image or video under the same case instructions.
5. Record time, retakes, instruction compliance, and participant-reported difficulty.
6. Have at least two independent adjudicators label whether the essential evidence deficit was resolved.
7. Resolve adjudicator disagreements using a pre-declared third-adjudicator rule; never relabel model output as human truth.

## Outcomes and analysis

Primary outcome: proportion of initially insufficient cases resolved by one follow-up capture.

Secondary outcomes:

- false support on cases adjudicated unresolved;
- abstention coverage;
- time and retake count;
- per-deficit resolution (blur, clipping, occlusion, missing final state);
- Cohen's kappa or Krippendorff's alpha for inter-rater agreement;
- sensitivity and specificity for detecting unresolved claims, with exact or Wilson intervals where sample support permits.

Analysis must be intention-to-treat, preserve the randomized assignment, report all exclusions, and keep the untouched test cases separate from any development threshold tuning. A small pilot should be labeled exploratory and should not be presented as validation.

## Stop and disclosure rules

- Stop if consent or media-rights requirements cannot be met.
- Do not infer physical causality from temporal order alone.
- Do not publish participant identities or raw private media.
- If participants are unavailable, report this protocol as ready but the study as **NOT COMPLETED**.
